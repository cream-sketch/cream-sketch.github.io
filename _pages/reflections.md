## Questions I Want to Answer by the End of My First Ph.D. Year

By the end of my first year, I want to be able to answer these questions and clearly explain my understanding, methods, and reflections to others.

1. **How do I read a paper?**
2. **Do I have a clear understanding of the Ph.D. timeline?**
3. **Have I published my first paper?**
4. **Have I developed a systematic way to organize literature and notes?**
5. **Am I eating well and sleeping well?**
