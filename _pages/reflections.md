## Questions I Want to Answer by the End of My First Ph.D. Year

By the end of my first year, I want to be able to answer these questions and clearly explain my understanding, methods, and reflections to others.

1. **How do I read a paper?** Can I clearly explain its motivation, key ideas, methodology, and limitations, as well as my own understanding of the work?
2. **Do I have a clear understanding of the Ph.D. timeline?** Do I know what I should prioritize now, what milestones lie ahead, and how the different stages fit together?
3. **Have I published my first paper?** It does not need to be at a top-tier venue. I believe the first paper matters less for prestige than for the confidence, reassurance, and momentum it provides during a journey that can often feel lonely.
4. **Have I developed a systematic way to organize literature and notes?** Can I efficiently retrieve, connect, and build upon what I have read?
5. **Am I eating well and sleeping well?** Am I taking care of myself while pursuing my research goals?
